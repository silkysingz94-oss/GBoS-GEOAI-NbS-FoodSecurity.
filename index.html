Methodology

1. Fetch daily climate series from Open-Meteo Archive for the selected period and sampling locations. 2. Aggregate daily values to monthly means/totals. 3. Request mapped wetland, mangrove, forest, woodland and protected-area features from OpenStreetMap Overpass. 4. Fetch selected annual national indicators from the World Bank API. 5. Display separate evidence streams; no causal claims are made.

Sources & definitions

Open-Meteo climate API; OpenStreetMap ecosystem/conservation features; World Bank national indicators. NDVI satellite imagery is not automatically loaded in this version; the vegetation option highlights mapped nature features, not measured NDVI.
